# INTRODUCTION
Finding job and internship opportunities can be challenging for university students, recent graduates, and other job seekers in Sierra Leone. Many people rely on newspapers, lecturers, friends, and personal connections to discover available opportunities. However, finding vacancy information through these different sources can be difficult, and it may not always be easy to know whether an opportunity is genuine or still available.
SierraWork is a proposed website designed to make job and internship opportunities more accessible by bringing them together on a single platform. The website will allow companies and organizations to submit vacancies for verification before publication. Job seekers will be able to browse verified opportunities, view job requirements, and apply through the application links provided.
The project focuses on designing a simple, user-friendly website that connects companies with potential candidates while helping job seekers access relevant opportunities. It will include five main screens: Home, Job Listings, Job Details, About Us, and Contact Us.
The purpose of this project is to apply web design and UI/UX principles to develop a website concept that addresses the challenges of discovering job opportunities and provides a more organized way for companies and job seekers to connect.

# PROJECT PROPOSAL
Project Title: SierraWork A Website for Verified Jobs and Internship Opportunities in Sierra Leone

Background
Finding employment and internship opportunities is an important part of building a career, especially for university students, recent graduates, and young people entering the job market.
In Sierra Leone, job seekers may discover opportunities through newspapers, lecturers, friends, personal connections, and other sources. However, information shared through these different channels can be difficult to find in one place. Some companies may also have limited online presence, making it harder for job seekers to find their vacancies.
Based on my own experience searching for internship opportunities, I recognized the need for a website that brings job and internship vacancies together in one accessible location.
SierraWork is a proposed website designed to help job seekers discover opportunities while giving companies a platform to share their vacancies with potential candidates.

Problem Statement
Job seekers, particularly university students and recent graduates, may face difficulties finding suitable job and internship opportunities. Vacancy information can be scattered across different sources, and it may be difficult to determine whether an opportunity is genuine and still available.
At the same time, companies need ways to reach potential candidates and share information about available positions.
Without a centralized platform that provides verified opportunities, job seekers may have difficulty accessing vacancy information, while companies may struggle to reach people looking for employment.


Proposed Solution
SierraWork will be a centralized website where companies can submit job and internship opportunities and job seekers can browse available vacancies.
The website will focus on three categories:
•	Internships
•	Full-time jobs
•	Part-time jobs
Companies will submit their vacancy information through a contact form or email. Before publishing an opportunity, the SierraWork team will verify the company and confirm that the specific vacancy is genuine and available.
Verified opportunities will display a Verified Opportunity badge to help job seekers identify listings that have passed the verification process.
Each listing will provide important information, including the company name, job title, location, description, requirements, qualifications, skills, and application deadline. Internship listings will also include the internship duration.
When a job seeker selects an opportunity, they can view its details and click the Apply for Job button. This button will direct them to a Google Form provided for that specific opportunity, where they can submit their application.
The website will provide a simple way for job seekers to discover opportunities and for companies to share vacancies without requiring users to create accounts.

USER NEEDS
The user needs are based on the problem I identified for SierraWork and the way we have designed the website.
JOB SEEKERS NEED
1. Easy access to opportunities
Users need a central place where they can discover internships, full-time jobs, and part-time jobs without having to search through many different sources.

2. Clear opportunity information
Users need important information about an opportunity before deciding whether to apply, including:
•	Company name and logo 
•	Job title 
•	Location 
•	Job description 
•	Requirements 
•	Qualifications 
•	Required skills 
•	Deadline 
•	Internship duration, where applicable 

3. Confidence in the opportunity
Users need to know that a vacancy is genuine and currently available. SierraWork addresses this through the Verified Opportunity badge.

4. Easy application access
Users need a straightforward way to apply once they find an opportunity. The Apply for Job button takes them to the relevant Google Form provided for that opportunity.

5. Opportunity filtering
Users need to be able to find the type of opportunity they are interested in. The Job Listings page therefore provides:
•	Internships 
•	Full-time Jobs 
•	Part-time Jobs 

COMPANIES NEED
1. A way to submit opportunities
Companies need a simple way to submit their vacancies through the website's contact form or email.
2. Access to potential applicants
Companies need a way to make their vacancies visible to people actively looking for employment and internships.
3. Accurate vacancy representation
Companies need their job information to be presented clearly so potential applicants understand the position before applying.

1. Main Navigation Menu
The navigation bar will contain four main links:
•	Home: Takes users to the homepage, where they can learn about SierraWork and see featured job opportunities.
•	Job Listings: Takes users to the page where they can browse and filter internships, full-time jobs, and part-time jobs.
•	About Us: Provides information about SierraWork, its purpose, and how it helps job seekers.
•	Contact Us: Allows companies to contact the website team to submit job opportunities and allows visitors to send enquiries.

2. Navigation Description
Users can access the four main pages through the navigation menu. When users select a job from the Job Listings page, they are taken to its Job Details page. From there, they can select the Apply for Job button, which opens the relevant Google Form to submit their application. The navigation structure is designed to be simple and consistent, allowing users to move between the main pages and find job opportunities without unnecessary steps.

# USER JOURNEY

Visit Home Page        Open Job Listings        Filter Opportunities         Select an Opportunity        View Job Details         Apply for Job         Complete Google Form
USER JOURNEY DESCRIPTION
The user begins by visiting the SierraWork homepage and navigates to the Job Listings page. They can filter opportunities by category and select a suitable job. After reviewing the job details and confirming that they meet the requirements, they click the Apply for Job button. This opens the relevant Google Form, where they complete and submit their application.

# CONCLUSION
The SierraWork website is designed to address the challenges faced by university students, recent graduates, and other job seekers in finding job and internship opportunities in Sierra Leone. Through user research, information architecture, and wireframing, the project focuses on creating a simple and user-friendly website that brings opportunities together in one place.
The website will allow users to browse internships, full-time jobs, and part-time jobs, view detailed information, and apply through Google Forms. Its verification process will help users identify opportunities that have been checked before publication.
Overall, this project demonstrates how web design and UI/UX principles can be applied to solve a real-world problem. SierraWork aims to make job opportunities easier to discover and help companies connect with potential candidates.
