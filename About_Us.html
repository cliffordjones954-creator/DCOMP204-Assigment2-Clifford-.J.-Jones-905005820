Connecting Young People

SierraWork is a Sierra Leonean platform dedicated to helping students, graduates and young people discover verified internships, full-time and part-time job opportunities from trusted organizations across the country.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between young talent and meaningful opportunities by providing a trusted and verified platform for job and internship listings in Sierra Leone.

Our Vision

A future where every young person in Sierra Leone has equal access to verified opportunities, empowering them to build successful careers and contribute to national development.

The Problem We’re Solving

Many students and young people struggle to find internships and job opportunities. Vacancies are often shared through scattered channels like newspapers, personal networks and companies that may have little or no online presence. This makes it difficult to know which opportunities are real and trustworthy.

  • Limited online presence from many companies
  • Information is scattered
  • Hard to know what is real and trustworthy

How Verification Works

We verify every opportunity before it goes live. Here’s how:

1

Company submits Opportunity

The company shares the job or internship details with us.

2

We Verify the Company

We confirm the company’s legitimacy and credibility.

3

We Verify the Vacancy

We confirm the opportunity is genuinely available.

4

Verified Opportunity Badge

The opportunity is marked with a verified badge on our website.

5

You Apply

You click “Apply” for the job and are redirected to the company’s Google Form.
